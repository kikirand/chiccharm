Our Story

Since 2021

Started in a spare bedroom. Still obsessed with fit.

ChicCharm began as a final-year fashion design project — a small capsule collection built to solve one problem: readymade clothing that actually fit the way it looked in the lookbook. Four years on, that same obsession with fit, fabric and finishing runs through every piece we make.

We design in small batches, test every fit on real bodies before it goes to production, and keep our supply chain close enough that we can answer questions about where something was made.

What We Stand For

Three principles that guide every collection.

01

Fit First

Every style is tested across five body types before it's approved for production — not just a single sample size.

02

Honest Pricing

No inflated "before" prices. What you see marked down is the real discount, calculated from our actual cost base.

03

Made to Last

We choose fabrics that hold colour and shape past the tenth wash, not just the first photo.

How We Got Here

A short timeline of a slightly chaotic, mostly rewarding journey.

2021

The First Collection

Twelve dresses, sewn by a two-person tailoring team, sold entirely through Instagram DMs.

2022

First Studio

Moved out of the spare bedroom into a proper design studio and hired our first full-time pattern master.

2023

Ethnic Wear Launch

Introduced our ethnic wear line, working directly with a family-run weaving unit for our saree fabric.

2024–26

Where We Are Now

Six category lines, a growing size range, and this website — built as a student project, run like a real one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message above.

How long does delivery take?
Orders are dispatched within 2 business days and typically arrive within 4–7 business days, depending on your location.
What is your return policy?
You can return or exchange any unworn item with tags attached within 15 days of delivery for a full refund or store credit.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available on all orders under ₹5,000 across serviceable pin codes.
How do I know which size to order?
Each product page includes a size guide with body measurements. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet — we currently ship across India only, but international shipping is on our roadmap.
